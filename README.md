# Youth Vybe

A social platform concept for teenagers aged 13-17, created as a team project at Notre Dame Catholic Sixth Form College.

## Project Overview

I worked in a team to design **Youth Vybe**, a safe social platform for teenagers. The fictional client was Futwell Technologies, a company focused on sustainability, and we were asked to bring young people into its work. Our research showed that around a third of young people aged 10-19 feel lonely some of the time, so we built a space where teenagers can make friends, join events, volunteer and take part in community activities.

## My Contributions

- **Safety features:** I worked on age verification, moderation, trusted contacts, privacy controls and blocking. Our age verification avoids asking for hard identifiers like passports unless a report makes it necessary, following ICO guidance.
- **Badges:** I helped design the badge system, which rewards participation, contribution and achievement instead of likes and popularity.
- **Surveys:** I sent out surveys to collect feedback on the badge designs. Users liked the creative names but wanted more colour and detail, and this fed into improvements.
- **Research:** I researched the investment side, including why sponsors and partners would back the platform, such as the summer tournament and what sponsors would get in return.
